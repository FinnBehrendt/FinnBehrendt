# Hi, I'm Finn
My goal is to turn promising medical imaging research into software clinicians can use in everyday practice. As Head of AI at [FUSE-AI](https://fuse-ai.de/ueber-uns), I lead the technical development of AI software for radiology. I consider clinical evidence and regulatory requirements throughout development, from designing the first experiments to deploying software in clinical settings.

Previously, as a Data Scientist in [HUK-COBURG’s Data Analytics program](https://www.huk.de/da), I trained fraud detection models for long-term care claims and built SQL pipelines for large claims datasets. Furthermore, I implemented and evaluated vision-language models to read medical and insurance documents and extract structured information in a local, privacy-aware setup.

During my PhD, I worked with our industry partner, [jung diagnostics](https://jung-diagnostics.com/index.html), and developed diffusion models to detect anomalies in brain MRI without training on annotated lesions. I work primarily with Python and PyTorch and care about reproducible experiments and rigorous model evaluation.

I'm also interested in language and vision-language models, evaluating retrieval systems, and building agents for recurring tasks in my own workflows. Depending on the task, I use local models or cloud APIs.
